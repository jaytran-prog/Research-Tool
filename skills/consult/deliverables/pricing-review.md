# Pricing Review

**Answers:** Are my prices and packages right — should I raise, lower, restructure, or leave them?
**Best for:** owners worried about margins, losing deals on price, or launching a new offer.

## Quick-form questions
1. What you sell and your current prices/packages (paste a link or list).
2. Rough costs or margin per sale, if you are willing to share.
3. Are you losing deals on price, struggling with margin, or both?
4. **(required)** Who is your typical customer, and which 3–5 alternatives do they compare you with? Give a link for each (website, pricing page, Google Maps, Facebook or delivery-app page) and any prices you already know.
5. Memo language?

## Workstreams
| id | Questions | Suggested sources | Budget |
|----|-----------|-------------------|--------|
| `price-benchmark` | What do comparable offers cost, at each tier, in this market? Where does the owner sit (low/mid/premium)? | Competitor pricing pages, marketplaces, menus, quotes in public listings | 6 searches · 10 fetches |
| `packaging` | How do competitors package and bill (tiers, bundles, subscriptions, add-ons, discounts, minimums)? | Competitor websites, marketplace listings | 5 searches · 6 fetches |
| `willingness-to-pay` | What do customers say about price and value? Complaints about price vs. complaints about quality. | Reviews, forums, Reddit, Facebook groups | 6 searches · 8 fetches |

## Critic checklist
- Benchmarks compare like-for-like offers (same scope, size, location).
- Every price is dated; promotional prices are labelled as such.
- Owner-provided costs are labelled as owner-provided.
- The recommendation's revenue impact is shown as a scenario with stated assumptions, not a forecast.
